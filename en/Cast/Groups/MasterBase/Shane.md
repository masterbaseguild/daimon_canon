# NEW

- aural, from the south-central regions (name to be decided)
- currently MasterBase quester
- fought as a vigilante in the suburbs of his hometown since a very young age
- moved to Hudel (on planet Cathum) to join the quester academy of the Kotel guild, but was disappointed with the training level reserved for his age
- pulled a stunt in order to be allowed in a more advanced class, impressing the teacher, who accepted him
- he defied the expectations of everyone and quickly became one of the best in his class
- this angered his classmates, and may have contributed to the incident where some of them killed the class teacher Anthras, who quite liked him
- traumatized after witnessing the teacher's body, the still very young shane went through months of rehab, and came out with heavy drug prescriptions
- since the color of blood would trigger instant traumatic responses in him, his quester career looked to be over, but his family, as a token of their support, gifted him a pair of color correcting glasses which would cancel out the color of blood and trick his mind
- in order to keep his mind focused he dedicated the following years of his life to the craft, graduating in record time and spending a couple months as a wandering quester before joining the masterbase in 2020
- despite communication issues, the masters grow fond of him and he gains some close friends who look out for his wellbeing
- his incredible discipline and productivity make him a very powerful asset for the guild, but this unsustainable lifestyle paired with his prescriptions lead to numerous injuries. while the masterbase higher ups tend to dismiss the issues (fearing they might lose one of the most proficient questers who ever fought under the masterbase banner), his friends help him wherever possible.
- weapons: whipsword, gun, veil of maya magic school (light based magic centered around illusions, stealth and invisibility)
- attire: long trench coat, color correcting glasses
- fighting style: assassin during large scale battles, but also versatile in special ops (intel gathering, assassinations, infiltrations etc)
- in the free time, he likes to keep himself busy by managing the Masteria bar and serving drinks to his friends
- he is also a skilled interior designer, and has worked on improving both the masterbase and the various colonies they founded, being one of the pillars of the masterbase building committee.
- specific activities he took part to under the masterbase banner:
	- took part to numerous operations on Iphiridia, assessing the situation for a potential future return of the MasterBase (after the 2017 Iphiridia disaster) and helping build attempted colonies in the more peaceful regions
	- took part to the second Master League, leading the AEV team

# OLD

**Shane** is a multifaceted member of **MasterBase**, whose presence is as valuable in the heat of combat as it is in the convivial atmosphere of the base's premises. He is a Questor who embodies the balance between the harshness of duty and the kindness of service.

---

## I. Physical Profile and Equipment
* **Race:** Aural.
* **Appearance:** Features a mulatto complexion and a *Lower Brush* hairstyle. Wear the official MasterBase uniform.
* **Armament:** A versatile fighter, he is equipped with a **Master Chainsword** and a **Master Gunblade**, combining brute strength and precision marksmanship.
* **Magic:** He is proficient in *Light Magic*, specializing specifically in the **Invisibility (Conceal)** spell, which he uses for infiltration maneuvers or to protect his companions.

## II. Combat Skills
* **Specializations:** He possesses a broad spectrum of technical skills, excelling in the use of blades, whips, and the art of *gunslinging* (via his Gunblade).
* **Role:** Within the guild, he plays a hybrid role of *Helper* and *Fighter*, making him extremely flexible in both offensive and logistical support missions.

## III. Background and Career
* **Entry into the Masters:** Shane joins MasterBase at a very young age, in 2020.
* **Field Experience:** He was a crucial element during the fortification of the colonies on **Iphiridia**, where he demonstrated unwavering dedication. Additionally, he served in fits and starts as the city guard of **Arken**, gaining tactical and patrolling experience.
* **Private Life:** Outside of duty, Shane cultivates a passion for the hospitality industry; he is known for managing bars and clubs, often acting as the official waiter at the **Masteria**, where he loves to take care of his companions.

## IV. Evolutionary Arc: The Value of Being
* **The Inner Conflict:** In his early years, Shane was a victim of toxic perfectionism. Driven by the praise of his classmates, he was convinced that social acceptance depended solely on his work productivity.
* **The Awareness:** His narrative arc led him to understand a fundamental truth: the respect and affection of his friends were not tied to his performance, but to his person. This revelation freed him from the need to "work himself to death," allowing him to be a more balanced and happy individual.
