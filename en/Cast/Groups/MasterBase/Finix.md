# NEW

- female of a humanoid race, born on a planet to be decided
- currently MasterBase quester
- the people in her home planet have developed the innate ability to shapeshift into huge birds, with enchantable feathers. her specific clan has specialized in fire magic, thus making their alternate form essentially a phoenix form.
- she leaves the village because of boredom and starts wandering, becoming a quester
- she joins spacefox, but mantains a selective approach and only works when she really wants to, preferring to spend her time wandering the ship and bothering everyone else
- despite that she unexpectedly bonds with Aledge, despite his strictness at the time, because he understands her personality and knows that she is more proficient when choosing her battles
- after spacefox falls, she wanders for a couple years, then aledge invites her to join the MasterBase, where she has some issues keeping the same lifestyle as before (because the MasterBase higher ups want her to be productive)
- when she first joined the MasterBase, she fell in love with a specific model of machinegun designed by Disti, and for a while she completely abandoned her Phoenix form so that she could instead use that, because "it was just so much fun". She then pivoted back to using her phoenix form after complaints from the rest of the guild members, who were mad at her careless way of using the weapon
- while in the MasterBase, she expands her arsenal with airbending magic, for turning her feathers into air bombs, which enhance her mobility, and organic magic, for allowing her feathers to emit a healing aura, which speeds up the natural cellular regeneration of nearby combatants
- in the free time, she sings on the stage of the Masteria, oftentimes lending her voice to Aledge's compositions

# Finix

**Finix** is a Questor of **MasterBase** who embodies a rare duality: deadly precision on the battlefield and a deep artistic talent that makes her the musical face of the guild. Ex-member of the **SpaceFox** guild, he found his new home and professional balance in the MasterBase.

---

## I. Physical Profile and System
* **Race:** Aural.
* **Appearance:** Dark skin contrasted by "flaming" red hair, characterized by a distinctive blond tuft. He regularly wears the MasterBase tactical uniform.

## II. Combat Profile
* **Armament:** Specialist in precision and saturation shooting, he uses a heavy **machine gun** with which he is able to control entire battle areas.
* **Magic:** Possesses a unique tactical combination:
* **Firebending:** Used for both offensive support and territory management via fire barriers.
* **Healing:** Thanks to his healing ability, he fills a vital role as a **Battle Healer**, ensuring that his frontline comrades can remain operational even under pressure.
* **Role:** Marksman / Battle Healer.

## III. History and Path
* **The SpaceFox Era:** He gained his initial experience in the ranks of *SpaceFox*, where he met **Aledge**. The bond established with him proved decisive for his future.
* **Joining MasterBase:** After the fall of *SpaceFox*, Finix joined Aledge at **MasterBase in 2020**, quickly integrating as a full member.
* **Social Expansion:** In 2022, he expanded his radius of influence by forging strong friendships with several members of the **Lignum Nobili**, enriching the guild's network of alliances.

## IV. The Voice of the MasterBase
* **Singing Talent:** Finix has exceptional vocal talents. He is an active member of the **Official MasterBase Band** and spends much of his free time at the *Masteria*, where his performances act as a social glue, bringing some warmth and comfort after the most arduous missions.
